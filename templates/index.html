Historic Vidarbha Philanthropy

The Noble Heritage of Sir Kasturchand Daga

Sir Kasturchand Daga was one of Central India's most revered philanthropists and industrialists, whose life was dedicated to social upliftment, education, healthcare, and charity throughout Nagpur, Kamptee, and the Vidarbha region.

Continuing this illustrious heritage, Sir Kasturchand Daga Bal Sadan in Lakad Ganj, Kamptee stands as a beacon of compassion. Here, children deprived of parental care find a warm family atmosphere, full educational funding, spiritual values, nutritious daily food, and hope for a bright future.
