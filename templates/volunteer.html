How You Can Make a Difference

Academic Mentoring & Tutoring

Help children in Classes 1 through 10 with Mathematics, Science, English, and homework assistance during study hours.

Conduct weekend creative workshops including drawing, origami, clay modeling, music, and moral value storytelling.

Sports, Yoga & Wellness

Lead energetic sports sessions (cricket, volleyball, kho-kho) and mindfulness yoga for physical wellness and team spirit.
