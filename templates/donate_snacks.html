IMPORTANT NOTICE FROM ORPHANAGE Timings & Guidelines

Why Monday to Saturday from 3:00 PM onwards?

The 45 children of Sir Kasturchand Daga Bal Sadan attend school in Kamptee during the morning and early afternoon. They return home around 2:30 PM, freshen up, and gather together at 3:00 PM for evening refreshments and snacks. Visiting during this window allows you to meet the children, distribute your treats personally, and enjoy their warm smiles and prayers!
